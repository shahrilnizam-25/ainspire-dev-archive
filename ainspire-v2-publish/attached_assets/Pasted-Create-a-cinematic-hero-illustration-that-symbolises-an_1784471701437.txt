⭐ One Improvement I Would Add

Since your product is called AiNspire, I would make the portal itself more meaningful.

Instead of simply showing someone walking into a portal, the portal should symbolise AI reasoning. As the employee enters, the portal subtly transforms them into an AI-empowered professional, while four coloured streams emerge from the other side, representing the four personas:

🔵 AI Explorer – Discover AI possibilities
🟢 AI Builder – Apply AI to improve work
🟣 AI Strategist – Drive organisational transformation
🟠 AI Visionary – Lead AI innovation
